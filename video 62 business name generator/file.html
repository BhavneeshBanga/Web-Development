01 — Image Quality pe seedha asar
Variable Default Kya karta hai & kya change hoga Impact
IMAGE_SIZE
 256 Image kitne pixels ki hogi training mein.
↑ Quality — 512 pe zyada detail ↑ VRAM — 4× zyada memory ↓ Speed — 4× slow

256 = standard. 512 try karo agar A100 GPU ho. 128 karo agar CPU pe ho.
HIGH
N_RESIDUALS
 9 Generator ke andar kitne Residual Blocks honge — ye model ki "sochne ki capacity" hai.
↑ 9 = 256px ke liye best ↓ 6 = 128px ke liye kaafi ↑ VRAM thoda badhta hai

Rule: 256px → 9 rakho. 128px → 6 karo.
HIGH
LAMBDA_CYCLE
 10.0 Cycle Consistency Loss ka weight — ye ensure karta hai ki Photo→Painting→Photo wapas original jaisi aaye.
↑ Badhao = content zyada preserve hoga ↓ Ghatao = style zyada aggressive hoga (blur risk) Stability ke liye 10 best hai

5–20 ke beech rakhna. Kam karo toh painting style strong hogi lekin artifacts aa sakte hain.
HIGH
LAMBDA_IDENTITY
 0.5 Identity Loss — colors preserve karta hai. Agar photo mein green tree hai toh painting mein bhi greenish rahe.
↑ Badhao = original colors maintain ↓ 0 karo = full artistic liberty (colors shift)

Portrait ke liye 0.5–1.0 rakho. Abstract art ke liye 0 try karo.
MED
02 — Training Time pe asar
Variable Default Kya karta hai & kya change hoga Impact
EPOCHS
 200 Kitni baar poora dataset dekhega model.
↑ Badhao = better results ↑ Training time directly proportional

Quick test ke liye 10–20. Decent results ke liye 50–100. Best ke liye 200.
200 epoch ≈ T4 GPU pe ~8–12 hours
MAX
BATCH_SIZE
 1 Ek baar mein kitni images process hongi.
↑ Badhao = thoda fast (marginal) ↑ VRAM bahut zyada laagta hai CycleGAN ke liye 1 BEST hai

Mat badlaao. CycleGAN paper khud 1 recommend karta hai — InstanceNorm batch=1 pe best kaam karta hai.
LOW
SAVE_EVERY
 5 Har N epoch ke baad checkpoint save hoga.
↑ Badhao = disk I/O kam, thoda fast ↓ Ghatao = zyada backups (disk space laagta hai)

Fast training ke liye 10–20 karo. Safe rehna hai toh 5 theek hai.
LOW
SAMPLE_EVERY
 1 Har N epoch ke baad sample image grid save hoga.
↑ 5 ya 10 karo = slightly faster training 1 = har epoch pe progress dekhte raho

Progress monitor karna ho toh 1. Speed chahiye toh 10 karo.
LOW
DECAY_EPOCH
 100 Kaun se epoch ke baad Learning Rate slowly 0 pe aane lagega.
↑ Badhao = zyada constant LR, faster early convergence ↓ Ghatao = jaldi fine-tune phase shuru

EPOCHS/2 rakho hamesha. 200 epochs → 100 decay. 50 epochs → 25 decay.
MED
03 — Learning Rate & Optimizer
Variable Default Kya karta hai & kya change hoga Impact
LR
 2e-4 Learning Rate — model kitni tezi se seekhega.
↑ Badhao = fast but unstable (mode collapse risk) ↓ Ghatao = stable lekin slow 2e-4 GAN ke liye golden value hai

Danger zone: 5e-4 se zyada mat karo — training crash ho sakti hai. 1e-4 safe alternative hai.
HIGH
BETA1
 0.5 Adam optimizer ka momentum parameter.
0.5 = GAN training ke liye standard 0.9 (default Adam) = GAN mein unstable hota hai

Mat badlaao. GAN literature mein 0.5 proven value hai.
MED
BETA2
 0.999 Adam ka second moment parameter — gradient variance track karta hai.
Default value almost always theek hai

Nahi badlna chahiye.
TINY

05 — Quick Summary
🖼️

Image Quality Badhani Hai?

  • IMAGE_SIZE 512 karo — sabse zyada farak
  • EPOCHS 200 full chalao — mat rokna
  • N_RESIDUALS 9 rakho (256px ke liye)
  • LAMBDA_CYCLE 10 — content blur hone se bachata hai
  • LAMBDA_IDENTITY 0.5 — colors realistic rakhta hai
⚡

Training Fast Karni Hai?

  • IMAGE_SIZE 128 — 4× faster + 4× less VRAM
  • EPOCHS 50 — decent results milenge
  • N_RESIDUALS 6 — 128px ke liye kaafi
  • SAMPLE_EVERY 10 — kam disk writes
  • SAVE_EVERY 10 — kam checkpoints
💾

VRAM Kam Hai (T4 / Free Colab)?

  • IMAGE_SIZE 256 rakho max — 512 mat karo
  • BATCH_SIZE 1 — already set hai, badlaao mat
  • N_RESIDUALS 6 karo thoda save
  • Mixed precision (fp16) enable karo Colab mein
🎯

Training Unstable Hai?

  • LR 1e-4 karo — 2e-4 se aadha
  • LAMBDA_CYCLE 10 → 15 badhao
  • BETA1 0.5 — mat badlaao
  • ReplayBuffer already set hai — ye help karta hai
  • DECAY_EPOCH pehle shuru karo (e.g. 75)
